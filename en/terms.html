3. Project Scoping, Quotes & Formation of Contract

Prior to any engagement, Jobydoo Agency provides a free, detailed written scope of work and quotation within 24 to 48 business hours upon receiving the client's specifications or discovery call.

The formal quotation clearly specifies:

  • The comprehensive list of technical features and deliverable assets;
  • Estimated milestone delivery timelines and sprint review intervals;
  • The fixed price in Moroccan Dirhams (MAD), Euros (EUR), or US Dollars (USD);
  • Quote validity duration (standard: 30 calendar days from issuance).
Binding Agreement: The contract becomes legally binding upon receipt by Jobydoo Agency of the signed quotation (electronic or handwritten) accompanied by the initial deposit payment.

4. Pricing, Currencies & Payment Milestones

All prices quoted are firm and documented in the accepted proposal. Unless otherwise specified in writing, custom software, web, and mobile projects follow our standard payment milestone schedule:

  • Initial Deposit: 30% to 50% upon contract signing to initiate discovery, UI/UX prototyping, and infrastructure setup;
  • Milestone Payment (for larger projects): 25% to 30% upon approval of interactive wireframes or functional staging build;
  • Final Balance: The remaining balance upon final acceptance testing and prior to public production release.

For ongoing Media Buying Retainers and maintenance plans, service fees are billed monthly at the beginning of each billing cycle. Ad spend budgets are paid directly by the client to advertising platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok).

Accepted Payment Methods: International bank wire transfer (SWIFT/SEPA), domestic Moroccan bank transfer, company cheque, or secure credit card processing (CMI / Stripe).

5. Intellectual Property & 100% Code Ownership Transfer

Our Guarantee to You: Unlike agencies that enforce restrictive licensing models or lock clients in proprietary hosting traps, Jobydoo Agency transfers 100% exclusive intellectual property ownership of the custom source code, designs, and databases to the Client upon full settlement of project invoices.

Upon full payment:

  • All custom code repositories, Figma designs, assets, and databases become the client's sole property with no mandatory recurring royalties;
  • The client retains complete administrative ownership of hosting servers, domains, and advertising accounts;
  • Standard open-source frameworks (React, Next.js, Flutter) remain under their respective permissive open-source licenses (MIT/Apache).

Jobydoo Agency reserves the legitimate right to reference completed public projects in its professional portfolio, unless a formal Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) explicitly excludes public attribution.

A successful digital build relies on timely collaboration. The Client agrees to:

8. Acceptance Testing, Delivery & Post-Launch Warranty

Upon completion of development, the solution is staged on a secure preview environment for User Acceptance Testing (UAT).

Acceptance Window: The Client has ten (10) business days to review the staging environment against the scope of work and report any defects or deviations. Once validated or after 10 days without written objection, the project is considered fully accepted.

Included Free Warranty: Every custom web, mobile, and CRM build delivered by Jobydoo Agency includes a complimentary 30 to 90-day technical warranty starting from launch. Any software bug or code discrepancy reported during this period is repaired promptly at zero additional cost.

The warranty does not cover issues caused by third-party hosting outages, external API modifications, or subsequent code tampering by the client or unvetted third parties.

9. Data Protection & Privacy (CNDP & GDPR)

Jobydoo Agency strictly complies with Moroccan Personal Data Protection Law (Law 09-08 / CNDP) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for international and European clients:

  • Data Minimization: Personal information submitted through our contact forms is used solely to respond to project inquiries and is never sold or shared with unauthorized third parties.
  • Security Standards: All databases, communications, and server endpoints utilize robust SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption and daily encrypted backups.
  • User Privacy Rights: Users and clients maintain full rights of access, rectification, and erasure regarding their data by contacting contact@jobydooagency.com.

Force Majeure: Neither party shall be held liable for delays or non-performance caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, nationwide telecommunication outages, armed conflicts, or government restrictions.

In the event of discretionary termination by the Client without fault on the Agency's part, all deposits paid remain with Jobydoo Agency, and all work completed up to the termination date shall be invoiced on a prorated basis.
