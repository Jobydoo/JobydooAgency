Why Server-Side Tracking (CAPI) Gives You a Unfair Advantage

Browser ad-blockers and Apple's iOS privacy updates blind standard browser pixels, losing up to 35% of your purchase and lead data. When ad algorithms can't see who converted, your cost per acquisition skyrockets.

We implement first-party server-side tracking (Meta CAPI & Google Enhanced Conversions) via Cloudflare / Stape. The ad platforms receive 100% of event signals, enabling the AI to optimize for your most profitable customer segments.
